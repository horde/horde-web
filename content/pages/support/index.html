Support

The Horde Project offers free community channels. As such we do not offer guaranteed response times. Look for a third party paid engagement if you need guaranteed response times or custom work.

Note that we can only address problems related to the Horde software itself. If you are having problems with any service using Horde software, for example your email account, please contact your service provider directly.

 Community Channels (free)

Horde is developed and supported by a community of volunteers. There's no guaranteed response time, but these are the right places to ask, report, or propose anything:

  • GitHub Issues and Pull Requests - code-level bugs, feature proposals, and patches, per-repository under github.com/horde.
  • bugs.horde.org - the project's own long-running bug tracker.
  • lists.horde.org mailing lists - developer discussion, feature requests, and user-to-user support.

See Community Support for more detail on how to get the most out of these channels.

 Paid Engagement

If you need guaranteed response times, custom feature development or help operating a large corporate Horde deployment, B1 Systems is a long-time Horde sponsor and a go-to point of contact for this kind of paid-for work.

Horde does not gatekeep who may offer paid Horde services. Any company or individual is free to build a consulting, hosting or integration business around Horde (see Commercial Use & Support for details on the licensing obligations this implies). We are glad to list your own commercial support venture or to welcome you as a sponsor.
