Commercial Use & Support

Free and open source - not "open core"

Horde does not sell an enterprise edition, a paid tier, or any feature that isn't already available upstream. Everything is released under GPL, LGPL, or BSD licenses (see Community for details). There is no vendor lock-in and nothing to unlock by paying anyone.

Need paid feature development or enterprise-scale operation?

B1 Systems is a long-time sponsor of the Horde Project and the established point of contact for organizations that need:

  • Commissioned feature development - funding specific new functionality that then ships upstream for everyone.
  • Operating or supporting a large corporate Horde deployment at enterprise service levels.

Anyone can build a business on Horde

Horde's licenses were chosen specifically to allow this: any company, agency, or individual developer is free to customize, extend, host, or consult on Horde for their clients or their own products. The project does not gatekeep who may offer Horde-based services, and does not compete with third-party consultancies or hosters. The only obligation is to comply with the license you're using - contributing back what GPL/LGPL/BSD require, nothing more. If you build something generally useful, upstreaming it (via a pull request - see Contribute) benefits everyone, including you, since it means you don't have to maintain a private fork forever.

Why Horde Groupware?

Horde is one of the most flexible groupware platforms available, and that makes it a solid foundation to build a business or a large deployment on:

  • Horde connects to (and officially supports) more backends (all the leading SQL, LDAP, IMAP, Sieve, SMTP, cache platforms available today) than any other open source groupware system. This means you can continue to use your existing userbase and your users do not have to remember any additional passwords.
  • Data entered in Horde may be updated or consumed via a wide range of front-end protocols (HTTP/HTML, DAV, SyncML, ActiveSync, iCalendar, more). Why let your data be siloed? Horde presents your information where you need it.
  • Template and CSS-based design makes customization easy. There are nearly unlimited numbers of ways to customize and configure Horde to fit almost any need.
  • Maturity. Horde's source code goes back over 20 years and benefits from years of engineering, bugfixes, security reports testing and user feedback.
  • Feature-rich. Horde's feature set rivals the largest enterprise email systems. We have an incredible feature set that compares favorably against any competition in our primary apps (mail, calendar, address book), and even more in our secondary apps (time tracking, wiki, ticketing, and more).
  • Open Source. Horde is open source, which means there are never per-user licensing fees or costly mandatory support contracts. It also means that, if necessary, you can customize any part of Horde at any time.
  • Active development. Horde benefits from an active community of developers and users on GitHub and lists.horde.org.
