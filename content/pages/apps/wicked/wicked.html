Wicked is a Wiki for the Horde framework. It uses PEAR's Text_Wiki package for markup rules, parsing, and rendering. It supports Creole, Mediawiki, Tiki Wiki, and its own markup engine.

Some of Wicked's features are:

  • Configurable markup engine
  • HTML, plain text, Latex, reStructuredText output rendering
  • Change tracking and revision history
  • Full-text searching
  • Per-page permissions
  • Attachments with versioning
  • Wiki synchronization

Wicked is also powering the official Horde Wiki.
