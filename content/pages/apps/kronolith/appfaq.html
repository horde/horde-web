Can Kronolith do resource booking (rooms, equipment)?

Yes. Kronolith has a built-in "Resources" feature for bookable rooms and equipment, including optional approval workflows (a resource owner can require confirmation before a booking is accepted) and conflict detection against existing bookings.

Can I sync my calendar with a phone, Thunderbird, or another CalDAV client?

Yes - Kronolith exposes calendars over CalDAV (and free/busy over iCalendar), so standard CalDAV clients (Apple Calendar, Thunderbird, most Android calendar apps) can subscribe to and sync Kronolith calendars directly, in addition to the native web UI.

Does Kronolith support shared/team calendars with per-user permissions?

Yes - calendars can be shared with other users or groups at varying permission levels (read, edit, delete, manage), the same permissions model used across Horde applications.

Can Kronolith handle recurring events with exceptions (e.g. "every Monday except public holidays")?

Yes - recurring events support standard iCalendar-style recurrence rules plus individual exceptions/overrides for single occurrences.
