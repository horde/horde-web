Can Nag remind me by email, not just show tasks in the web UI?

Yes - Nag can send email reminders for upcoming/overdue tasks, in addition to surfacing them in the web UI and the Horde portal.

Can I share a task list with my team, with some tasks assigned to specific people?

Yes - task lists can be shared (same Horde-wide permission model as Kronolith/Turba), and individual tasks can be assigned to a specific user.

Does Nag support recurring tasks (e.g. "every first Monday of the month")?

Yes - tasks support recurrence rules similar to Kronolith's calendar events, not just one-off due dates.
