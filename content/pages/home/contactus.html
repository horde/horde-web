Contact Info

The best way to get in touch with The Horde Project and it's community depends on your needs. We have the following options:

Email/IRC

We have numerous mailing lists to help answer your questions, and are also availble on the #horde channel on freenode. See the support page for more details on IRC.

Security Concerns

If you wish to contact us regarding a potential security concern. Please see our security reporting procedures at https://wiki.horde.org/SecurityManagement

Consulting Services

If you are looking to contact us about possible consulting servcies or other custom work, you may contact us at info@horde.org. You can also view our Services page for more information.
